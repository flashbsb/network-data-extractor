📖 Network Intelligence Metrics Legend

⚠️ Recent Anomalies Identifies historical standard deviation spikes. Shows recent connections where measured latency or loss significantly exceeded the baseline average.
🔥 Top 10 Worst Circuits Aggregates circuits with the highest average packet loss and latency over the last 10 snapshots. Useful for highlighting consistently poor-performing connections.
📉 Degradation Trend (Regression) Applies linear regression analysis over time. Circuits with a positive coefficient (+) are progressively worsening (latency or loss increasing per run).
📊 Instability Ranking (Std Dev) Sorts circuits by standard deviation (σ) of latency. High values indicate highly unstable circuits with jitter and performance fluctuations.
